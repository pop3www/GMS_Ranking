Supplementary Figure 1 source-data traceability
===============================================
The manuscript-used Figure S1 display is included in final_outputs/FigureS1.pdf.
The exact processed promoter-profile tables were not present in the private release tree and are therefore not claimed as bundled source data.
The underlying ChIP-seq is traceable to GEO accession GSE318271, and the RNA-seq rank-band assignment is traceable to GSE318584 (SuperSeries GSE318586).
If GEO is private during review, anonymous reviewer tokens are supplied confidentially through Editorial Manager and are never stored in GitHub.
See GEO_TRACE.tsv for assay-level provenance and reproduction routing.
